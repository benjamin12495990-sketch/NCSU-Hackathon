Data-quality traps
Bradley includes values marked "do not use". Filter them out, and keep melting points in kelvin.

Bradley's compounds are keyed by ChemSpider ID, so you need your own structure matching.

SolProp mixes 298 K aqueous values with organic-solvent values at other temperatures. Select only the aqueous logSaq subset.

Do not feed SolProp's computed or derived property columns (such as Abraham parameters) to your model as if they were measured.
