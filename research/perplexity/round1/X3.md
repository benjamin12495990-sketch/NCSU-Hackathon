Merging cautions
Concrete: store specimen shape, size and curing as separate fields. The sources show the ratio changes with strength level, so a single multiplier is risky.

Hardness: keep HV and HIT as distinct fields. The reported differences depend on load and depth, and a size effect exists in both tests.

Tg: store the method (DSC midpoint, onset, tan δ, E″, E′ onset), the heating rate or frequency, and whether it was a first or second heat. The unrated PET and heating-rate examples are illustrative only.

Gaps: I found no sourced numbers for thermodynamic vs kinetic solubility, weight loss vs LPR, Voigt vs Reuss vs Hill, PBEsol moduli, or HSE vs measured optical gap beyond the MAE row above. Primary papers would be needed for those.
