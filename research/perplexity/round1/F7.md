Because of this, tasks 1, 2 and 4 are largely unanswered. The Li-ion paper itself says it dropped activation energy because it was not reported often enough.

Mixed temperatures: LiIon holds 820 entries at 5–873 °C, with 403 unique compositions near room temperature. OBELiX cites 465 room-temperature entries. Filter to 15–35 °C before merging.

Duplicates: OBELiX has a Liion ID column that points back into LiIon. Dedupe on that column before merging.

Liquid electrolyte datasets: The Zenodo 7244939 record covers liquid electrolytes (5,035 experiments). It does not belong in a solid-electrolyte set.

Computed against measured: The stability-window databases above are computed. Mixing them with measured values would be a trap.
